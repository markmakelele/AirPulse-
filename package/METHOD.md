# AirPulse™ Service Design Kit

A reusable methodology for moving from journey evidence to service requirements.

1. Understand — journey and behaviour
2. Discover — pain points and needs
3. Align — stakeholder workshop
4. Prioritize — impact × feasibility
5. Design — service blueprint
6. Orchestrate — relative Gantt
7. Prototype — multimodal interaction
8. Validate — research and testing
